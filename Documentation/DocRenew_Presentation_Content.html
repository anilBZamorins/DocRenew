The Problem

Context

On the slide

Documents expire quietly — until they cost you.

  • Legal & regulatory risk — an expired visa or permit means fines, failed audits, and a workforce no longer cleared to work.
  • Operational disruption — a lapsed certification pulls a driver, nurse, or operator off the job, halting work with no warning.
  • Manual, reactive tracking — HR chases deadlines by hand across scattered files; it doesn't scale and fails silently.

Tracked per employee: Passports · Visas · Work Permits · Licenses · Certifications

Say this

“Every organization manages critical documents for its people — passports, visas, work permits, licenses, certifications. The problem is that these expire quietly. When even one is missed, the consequences are real: legal penalties and failed audits, operations disrupted because someone is no longer cleared to work, and HR teams stuck tracking everything manually. That manual tracking is the root issue — it doesn't scale, and it fails without anyone noticing until it's too late.”

How It Works

Workflow

On the slide

Escalating reminders on a proven timeline.

WhenWhoPurpose
3 MonthsEmployee & CompanyBegin the renewal process early
1 MonthEmployee & CompanyFollow-up if still pending
5 DaysEmployee & CompanyUrgent — avoid expiration
On ExpiryEmployee & CompanyExpired — act immediately
After ExpiryManagementEscalation for overdue renewals

Delivered by: Email · SMS · Mobile App

Say this

“The heart of the system is an escalating reminder timeline. Three months before a document expires, both the employee and the company get an early reminder. One month out, a follow-up if it's still pending. Five days before, an urgent alert. On the expiry date, an immediate action notice — and if it still isn't handled, it escalates to management. Every reminder goes out across email, SMS, and mobile app, so nothing gets buried in an inbox.”

Prepare for questions

Quick Q&A prep

How are the reminder times set?
They're configurable per document type; the defaults are 3 months, 1 month, 5 days, on expiry, and after expiry.
Who receives the alerts?
Employees and the company in parallel; overdue cases escalate to management.
What notification channels are used?
Email, SMS, and mobile/app push notifications.
Who can access what?
Role-based access — Admin, HR, Manager, and Employee, each with the right view.
Where does it run?
A cloud-based SaaS platform, available on web and mobile.
