The Problem

Compliance documents expire quietly — until they cost you.

Passports, driving licenses, work permits, visas, and certifications all have deadlines. Most companies still track them in spreadsheets and memory. Something always slips.

Legal & regulatory risk

An expired visa or permit can mean fines, failed audits, and a workforce that's no longer allowed to work.

Operational disruption

A lapsed certification pulls a driver, nurse, or operator off the job — halting projects with no warning.

Manual, reactive tracking

HR chases deadlines by hand across scattered files. It doesn't scale, and it fails silently.

The Solution

One system that watches every deadline for you.

DocRenew is a centralized, secure repository for employee documents. It automatically tracks expiry dates and sends timely, escalating reminders to both the employee and the company — well before anything lapses.

  • Proactive, not reactive. Renewals start on time, automatically.
  • Everyone stays accountable. Employees and HR are notified in parallel.
  • Full visibility. A live dashboard of upcoming, expired, and renewed documents.
